# Rhyolite Sync

Keep the same notes and files on your devices with end-to-end encryption.

- **[Connect Obsidian](OBSIDIAN.md#installation)** to sync your vault between your computer and phone.
- **Sync a folder without Obsidian (coming with the first headless release)** to keep a working copy on a NAS or server. Edits travel in both directions.
- **[Run your own sync server](SELFHOST.md)** to host the encrypted data for your devices on your infrastructure.

Both clients work with Rhyolite Cloud or your own server. Cloud uses a Rhyolite account and offers paid plans.
